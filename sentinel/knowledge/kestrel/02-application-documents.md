# Kestrel Credit Union: What to Bring

## Identity
One government photo ID. If you do not have one, two of: a birth certificate, a utility bill in your name, or a letter from an employer or school.

## Income
- Employees: your 2 most recent payslips.
- Self-employed and gig workers: your most recent tax return, or 3 months of bank statements showing regular income.
- Students: a letter confirming any stipend, scholarship or part-time work.

## Bank statements
3 months of statements from your main account.

## Co-signers
If you apply with a co-signer, they need the same identity and income documents and must be a Kestrel member.

## Timeline
Automatic decisions are instant. Lending officer reviews are usually completed within 1 business day. Funds are paid into your Kestrel account the same day you sign.
