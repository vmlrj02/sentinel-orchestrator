# Kestrel Credit Union: Declines, Appeals and Next Steps

## Understanding the decision
We explain every decision in plain language, including the factors that most increased and decreased your estimated risk.

## Appeals
You can ask for a review within 14 days. A different lending officer looks at your application, and you can add information the model could not see, such as a new job offer or a co-signer. Reviews take up to 3 business days.

## Reapplying
You can reapply after 30 days, or sooner if your circumstances change materially (for example, a new job or a co-signer).

## Improving your chances
- Borrow less relative to your income; under 30% of annual income is ideal.
- Add a co-signer with a longer credit history.
- Use our Credit Builder loan: a small $500 loan held in savings while you repay it, which builds credit history in 6 to 12 months.
- Choose debt consolidation if you have several debts, which also earns a rate discount.

## Free financial counselling
Every declined applicant is offered a free session with a certified financial counsellor. Book through the member app or by replying to your decision message.
