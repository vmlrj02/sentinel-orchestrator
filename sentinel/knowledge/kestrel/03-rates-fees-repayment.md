# Kestrel Credit Union: Rates, Fees and Repayment

## Interest rates by loan grade
Rates are fixed and set by the board each quarter.
- Grade A: 6.9% to 8.9%
- Grade B: 8.9% to 11%
- Grade C: 11% to 13%
- Grade D: 13% to 15.5%
- Grade E: 15.5% to 18%
- Grade F: 18% to 21%
- Grade G: 21% to 24%
Debt consolidation loans receive a 1 percentage point discount.

## Fees
- No origination fee.
- No early repayment fee, ever.
- Late payment fee: $15, waived once per year on request.

## Terms
Terms from 6 to 60 months. You can repay weekly, fortnightly or monthly to match your pay cycle.

## Hardship programme
If your circumstances change, our hardship programme can pause payments for up to 3 months or extend your term. Contact a lending officer before you miss a payment.
