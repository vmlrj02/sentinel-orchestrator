# Kestrel Credit Union Lending Policy

## Who can apply
Kestrel is a member-owned credit union. Anyone aged 18 or over can join for a one-off $5 membership fee and apply for a loan the same day. We welcome applicants with a thin credit file: if you have less than 1 year of credit history, you can apply with a co-signer.

## How much you can borrow
Personal loans range from $500 to $30,000. The loan amount must not exceed 50% of your gross annual income. First-time members can borrow up to $10,000 in their first year of membership.

## How decisions are made
Applications are scored by a credit risk model that estimates the probability of default.
- Estimated default risk below 40%: approved automatically.
- Estimated default risk from 40% to 70%: reviewed by a member lending officer, usually within 1 business day.
- Estimated default risk above 70%: declined, with a referral to our free financial counselling service.
Loans above $15,000 are always reviewed by a lending officer.

## Previous defaults
We consider applicants with a previous default. If the default is more than 2 years old and settled, our lending officers can give it less weight than the model does.

## Loan purposes we support
Personal, education, medical, home improvement, debt consolidation and venture loans. Debt consolidation loans get a 1 percentage point rate discount because they usually reduce members' total monthly payments.
