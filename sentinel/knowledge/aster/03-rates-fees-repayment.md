# Aster Bank: Rates, Fees and Repayment

## Interest rates by loan grade
Your loan grade reflects your credit profile. Annual interest rates are fixed for the life of the loan.
- Grade A: 7.5% to 9.5%
- Grade B: 9.5% to 12%
- Grade C: 12% to 14.5%
- Grade D: 14.5% to 17%
- Grade E: 17% to 20%
- Grade F: 20% to 23%
- Grade G: 23% to 26%

## Fees
- Origination fee: 1.5% of the loan amount, deducted from the payout.
- Late payment fee: $25 per missed payment.
- Early repayment: free after the first 12 months; before that, a fee of 1% of the amount repaid early.

## Terms
Loan terms are 12, 24, 36, 48 or 60 months. Repayments are monthly by direct debit.

## Missed payments
If you expect to miss a payment, contact us before the due date. We can move your payment date once per year at no cost. Two consecutive missed payments are reported to credit bureaus.
