# Aster Bank: If Your Application Is Declined

## Understanding the decision
Every decision comes with the main factors that raised and lowered your estimated risk. The most common reasons for a decline are a high loan amount relative to income, a short credit history, a previous default, and a low loan grade.

## Appeals
You can appeal a decline within 30 days by replying to your decision message or emailing appeals@asterbank.example. An underwriter who was not involved in the original decision reviews the appeal within 5 business days. Include any information the model could not see, such as a recent pay rise or a settled default.

## Reapplying
You can reapply after 90 days. Applying more often does not improve your chances and each application may appear on your credit file.

## How to improve your chances
- Borrow less: keeping the loan below 25% of your annual income has the biggest effect on risk.
- Build credit history: keep accounts open and in good standing; history of 4 years or more lowers risk noticeably.
- Stay in your job: employment of 2 years or more with the same employer is viewed favourably.
- Settle defaults: a settled default weighs less than an outstanding one.
- Consider a joint application with a co-borrower who has a stronger profile.

## Complaints
If you believe a decision was unfair, you can raise a complaint at complaints@asterbank.example. We respond within 15 business days.
