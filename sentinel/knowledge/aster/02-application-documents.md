# Aster Bank: Documents You Need

## Identity and address
- A government photo ID (passport, driving licence or national ID card).
- Proof of address dated within the last 3 months, such as a utility bill or bank statement.

## Income
- Salaried applicants: your last 3 monthly payslips.
- Self-employed applicants: your last 2 years of tax returns and 6 months of business bank statements.
- Other income (rent, pension, benefits) must be shown on a bank statement or official letter.

## Bank statements
Your last 6 months of personal bank statements from the account your salary is paid into.

## For specific loan purposes
- Education: an admission letter or fee schedule from the institution.
- Medical: a cost estimate or invoice from the provider.
- Home improvement: a contractor quote. If you rent, a letter of consent from your landlord.
- Debt consolidation: statements for each debt you want to consolidate.

## How long it takes
Once all documents are uploaded, automatic decisions are instant. Referred applications are decided within 2 business days. Funds are paid out within 1 business day of signing the loan agreement.
