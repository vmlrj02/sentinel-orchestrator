# Aster Bank Personal Lending Policy

## Who can apply
Applicants must be at least 21 years old and no older than 70 at the end of the loan term. You need a regular income and at least 2 years of credit history with a recognised credit bureau. Aster Bank does not accept applications from people without a credit history; we recommend building one with a secured card first.

## How much you can borrow
Personal loans range from $1,000 to $40,000. The loan amount must not exceed 40% of your gross annual income (loan-to-income ratio). For example, an applicant earning $50,000 a year can borrow up to $20,000. Applications above 40% are declined automatically, even if the credit model scores them as low risk.

## How decisions are made
Every application is scored by our credit risk model, which estimates the probability of default from your income, employment length, home ownership, loan amount, interest rate, loan grade, loan purpose, credit history length and any previous default.
- Estimated default risk below 30%: approved automatically, subject to the loan-to-income limit.
- Estimated default risk from 30% to 60%: referred to a human underwriter, who decides within 2 business days.
- Estimated default risk above 60%: declined.
Any loan above $20,000 is always reviewed by an underwriter, whatever the model score.

## Previous defaults
A default recorded in the last 5 years does not automatically disqualify you, but it is a significant risk factor in the model. Underwriters may ask for a written explanation and evidence that the debt was settled.

## Loan purposes we support
Personal, education, medical, home improvement, debt consolidation and venture (small business) loans. Venture loans above $10,000 require a short business plan.
